## Platform disclaimer (long form)

The platform provides non-recommendatory quantitative analytics, historical
statistics, and market-structure features for independent strategy research and
risk-model enhancement. The platform does not provide investment advice, research
recommendations, buy/sell/hold opinions, price targets, entry/exit instructions,
model portfolios, personalized suitability-based advice, or execution
instructions. Because SEBI's definition of research report / research services
may cover opinions concerning securities that provide a basis for investment
decisions, this product must be reviewed by SEBI-specialist counsel before any
paid public launch.

## Output disclaimer (short form, carried on every response)

This output is non-recommendatory market analytics for informational and research
use only. It is not investment advice, not a buy/sell/hold recommendation, not a
price target, and not an instruction to trade. Users are solely responsible for
independent validation and decisions. Historical statistics do not guarantee
future outcomes.

## Scope of use

- Informational / research use by sophisticated users only.
- B2B / user-owned-strategy context; outputs are inputs to a user's own
  independent process, never a decision made for the user.
- Redistribution or resale of the feed is prohibited by the terms of service.

See `docs/sebi_compliance_notes.md` for the internal positioning, the enforced
language policy, and the open legal/regulatory actions.
